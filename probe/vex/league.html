Vex Volleyball 2027 Winter League

Play in one of Austin’s most play-heavy indoor volleyball leagues.

Every team is guaranteed two matches per night, with each match played 2 sets.

The league will run for 6 weeks: 5 weeks of league play, one week of playoffs.

Are you a free agent? Don’t worry, we have an option for free agents to sign up on a list for existing captains to pick from below. Use the same link to sign up and select the free agent option!
