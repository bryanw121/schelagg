Dec. 6-Foot and Under Tourney

12/5/2026

Register Here
Team/Free Agent List

We’re excited to bring back our 6-Foot and Under Tournament with Santa’s Elves this December! Compete and show out! Men above 6 feet may be eligible to play Libero only. Women do not have a height restriction.

To help keep the event running smoothly, we’re implementing a $75 team deposit for reffing responsibilities, which will be fully refunded once your team completes their assigned reffing duties.

To help keep the event running smoothly, we’re implementing a $75 team deposit for reffing responsibilities, which will be fully refunded once your team completes their assigned reffing duties.

Team entry is only confirmed once payment is made and all players are registered on the Google Form. Be sure to include your team name in the payment notes.

Tournament registrations are generally non-refundable. If a team withdraws and the spot can be filled, a refund or event credit may be issued at Vex Volleyball's discretion. Team transfers are permitted with organizer approval.

    1. No rating restrictions

    2. Iron man middle is allowed

  • Pool Play

    • Timed: 45 minute matches (include 5 minute shared warm-up)

    • Each Match consist of 2 sets

    • Each set to 25, start at 4,

      • Deuces is win by 2 but cap at 27

    • If there is a tie after match end time, 1 more point will be played to determine the set winner

    • Each team has two 30-second time-outs per set.

    Tournament Play (Playoffs)

    • Single Elimination

    • Timed: 60 minute matches (include 5 minute shared warm-up)

    • Each match is best 2 out of 3

    • First two set to 25, start at 4,

      • Deuces is win by 2 but cap at 27

    • 3rd set to 15 points, start at 0. Winner determined by team with more points at the end of 60 minute or to 15 points

      • Deuces is win by 2 but cap at 17

    • Each team has two 30-second time-outs per set

  • Q: Can I have a new sub if a player no shows/gets injured?

    A: Yes, as long as they meet the tourney rules and restrictions.

    Q: Can I borrow a sub from another team?

    A: No, this is not allowed.

    Q: Can I bring a sub into playoffs?

    A: No, please make sure your roster has an appropriate amount of players to account for this.
